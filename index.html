About GlassBench

GlassBench is a community-driven platform dedicated to smart glasses, XR technologies, spatial computing, and wearable intelligence.

The platform brings together product comparisons, technical glossaries, hardware teardowns, unboxing resources, and community contributions to make emerging technologies easier to explore and understand.

Whether you're discovering your first pair of smart glasses, learning how waveguides work, comparing devices, or exploring the technology behind modern XR systems, GlassBench aims to provide a clear and accessible place to learn.

Built by an enthusiast passionate about the future of computing, GlassBench exists to help create a more informed community around the technologies shaping the next generation of human-computer interaction.
